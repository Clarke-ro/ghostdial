# GhostDial
An AI study tutor that works when your data runs out. Users dial *266# (BOO on a keypad) and navigate numbered USSD-style menus on a retro phone in the browser.
- Audience: students who run out of data or signal, plus anyone with no internet access.
- Features: Ask the Oracle (AI Q&A), Quiz Me, Exam Survival tips, Explain Like I'm 5, My Haunt Score.
- Tone: a haunted old phone line. Spooky but friendly, plain simple English.
