# Style
- Generic retro candy-bar phone. No brand names or logos (no "Nokia", no real ringtones).
- Green-on-dark LCD screen, pixel font (VT323), scanlines, glow, slight flicker.
- Dark charcoal/purple phone body with faint cobwebs and a purple glow.
- Keypad beep sounds, and static while loading.
- Status bar shows signal bars and a "No Data" badge.
